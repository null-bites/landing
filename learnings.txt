## 3Dmol notes
- MUST dynamic-import with `ssr: false`; the lib touches window at import time.
- Spin via `viewer.spin('y', <speed>)`. Wrap host div in `pointer-events-none` so scroll isn't trapped.
- PDB candidates: 1HHO (hemoglobin, iron/nutrition tie-in), 1OVA (ovalbumin), 1CRN (tiny fallback).
